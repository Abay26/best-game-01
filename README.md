Lumen Harvest — a neon arena survival game across five realms: pilot a harvester drone, collect lumen motes to build a combo multiplier, slay eight wisp types plus a boss in each realm, dodge each realm's own hazard, and clear all ten waves of Ember Wastes, Frost Hollow, Void Garden, Storm Reef and the new Aurora Tide. Harvesting and slaying fill the overdrive meter — at full, Q fires a surge of faster fire, faster flight and piercing shots. Kill streaks without taking a hit pay bonuses, chain lightning zaps the nearest wisp every sixth kill, and an aegis shield blocks one hit per wave. The armored warden tanks hits from wave 5 on; from wave 4 on, the first wisp of a wave may come up elite — double health, double score, a gold ring, and a heart on the line. About one mote in eight is golden, worth triple. A 25 combo sets off Frenzy: six seconds of doubled score. Clearing all five realms opens Endless Tide, waves without end in a random realm. Clearing a realm opens the next on the realm map; best scores, best combos and cleared realms are saved between visits. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best game" bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
